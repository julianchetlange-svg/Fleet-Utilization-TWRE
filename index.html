🔮 Growth & Reallocation What-If

Conservative, evidence-bounded model

Models the combined effect of delivery volume growth and asset reallocation from underused ("cold") stores to stores closer to utilization goals ("hot" stores). Mi/delivery and cost/delivery improvements are bounded by what this fleet has actually demonstrated historically, not speculative extrapolation.

Growth Scenario: 0% Blend: move what growth needs, eliminate the rest

Assets under 20% utilization are flagged for reallocation, with a 36% post-removal store cap, subject to: Store-to-Door Pickups are never removed. Every store keeps a minimum of 1 Pickup, 1 20' Slide, and 1 24' TA Flatbed or Tractor TA. The five stores near 355CD shift fully to central delivery — their core delivery assets are reallocated to 355CD, reallocate to a hot store, or removed from the fleet.

Methodology: Density/cost figures use trucks+pickups, quarterly-averaged fleet counts. Ceiling = 2022 (870.6/asset), the best year on record. Track 1 surplus = individual util <20% with a 36% post-removal store cap; Track 2 = Region 05 stores routed through 355CD. Update status: Fixed and variable fleet cost, deliveries, weight utilization, and duration are fully real for all 7 months (Jan–Jul), each month verified against its own source files. Known approximation: 10 assets that left the fleet before July (598005, 692008, 748051, 750024, 750028, 750032, 750040, 750051, 752015, 754011) were excluded from the Weight Utilization source files' own matching (matched against July's roster, not each month's own), so their real historical deliveries (~3,117, roughly 1.5% of the fleet total) were recovered from aggregate unmatched-asset counts and spread evenly across their active months — not true dated data. A corrected re-pull matched against each month's own roster would replace this. Track 1/Track 2 membership and hot-store ranking order still reflect the original selection — re-deriving those from scratch produced 3–4x more surplus assets than the original list even when tested against the old data, indicating the reproduction is missing a constraint, not that the fleet changed that much. Treat membership/ranking as unconfirmed until the original selection logic is available to validate against.
