Methodology: Fixed and variable fleet cost, deliveries, weight utilization, and duration are fully real for all 9 months (Jan–Sep), each month verified against its own source files; September was processed from the raw Weight Utilization export matched against the September roster. Fuel is rebuilt from raw Wex transactions for every month (Total Fuel Cost, each asset's fuel to its home store, generic-card fuel spread evenly across each store's assets). Known approximation: 10 assets that left the fleet before July (598005, 692008, 748051, 750024, 750028, 750032, 750040, 750051, 752015, 754011) were excluded from the Jan–Aug Weight Utilization source files' own matching (matched against a later roster, not each month's own), so their real historical deliveries (~3,117, roughly 1.5% of the fleet total) were recovered from aggregate unmatched-asset counts and spread evenly across their active months — not true dated data. Duration, trips/day, and % days active could not be similarly recovered for these assets. 752015 returned to the roster in September.
