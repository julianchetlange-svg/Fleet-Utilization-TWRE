Methodology: Fixed and variable fleet cost, deliveries, weight utilization, and duration are fully real for all 8 months (Jan–Aug), each month verified against its own source files. Known approximation: 10 assets that left the fleet before July (598005, 692008, 748051, 750024, 750028, 750032, 750040, 750051, 752015, 754011) were excluded from the Weight Utilization source files' own matching (matched against a later roster, not each month's own), so their real historical deliveries (~3,117, roughly 1.5% of the fleet total) were recovered from aggregate unmatched-asset counts and spread evenly across their active months — not true dated data. A corrected re-pull matched against each month's own roster would replace this. Duration, trips/day, and % days active could not be similarly recovered for these assets, since the Weight Utilization source files never captured their raw activity at all.
